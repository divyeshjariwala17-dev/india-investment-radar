FIRST TIME — TOMORROW
---------------------
1. Extract the ZIP to:
   C:\India_Investment_Radar

2. Double-click:
   setup_and_run.bat

3. Your browser opens.

4. On the LEFT click:
   FIRST SETUP / FULL DATA
